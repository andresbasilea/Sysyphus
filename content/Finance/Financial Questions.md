### What is the purpose of finance in an economy supposed to be? Is it still performing that role?

- Finance has historically been the intermediate between capital and other factors of production (land, labor)
- Finance industry takes money (capital), and allocates it to productive economic activities (labor, land, entrepeneurship) to generate a ROI. 
- For example, investing capital on creating housing on land; or investing capital on entrepeneurs that have the ability to produce of new goods and services.
