Provides necessary and sufficient conditions for a market to be **arbitrage-free**, and for a market to be **complete**. 


It implies that in a [complete market](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complete_market "Complete market"), a derivative's price is the discounted [expected value](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Expected_value "Expected value") of the future payoff under the unique risk-neutral measure. Such a measure exists [if and only if](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/If_and_only_if "If and only if") the market is arbitrage-free
