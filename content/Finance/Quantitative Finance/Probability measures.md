
Measure: Assign how much each subset of X measures. 

$$
\mu(\emptyset) = 0
$$


$$
\mu (E)  \ge 0
$$

There are subsets that cannot be measured. We focus on studying those who are measurable (**sigma algebra**).

A **sigma algebra** is a collection of subsets of X. All of the sets that can be assigned a measure (the measure devuelve a real number). The most common one is Borel´s. 

A measure is generally defined over a sigma algebra. 

A probability measure is a measure with an extra condition: 
- The values it gives back must be between [0,1]. 
- The measure of the whole set must be 1.
